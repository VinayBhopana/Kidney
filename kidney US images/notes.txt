checking diff preprocessing methods to find the best one 
greyscale , bilateral filter , clahe , bilateral+clahe 

Grayscale: preserves the original ultrasound signal best.
CLAHE: improves local renal contrast noticeably, but also amplifies speckle/noise.
Bilateral: reduces some noise, but the effect is mild and may smooth small echogenic stone features.
Bilateral + CLAHE: visually strong, but I would not make this the default yet because denoising can potentially suppress small calculi.

Baseline = grayscale only
Preprocessing ablation = CLAHE

The key sequence was 9,416 original → 1,040 exact duplicates removed → 8,376 retained → strict near-duplicate grouping → group-aware 70/15/15 split → grayscale → 224×224.
